Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Download the app, tap Registrujte se (Sign up) and enter your first name, last name, email and password. Then add your child on the Moj paket (My package) tab.

Where is my child's QR code?

Tap the round QR button in the middle of the bottom bar and choose your child. Show the code at reception on arrival and on departure.

My remaining hours look wrong.

The balance updates as soon as staff scan the QR code. If it still doesn't match, ask the playroom staff or email us — the playroom's records are authoritative.

I forgot my password.

Email us at kidsclubbw@gmail.com from the address the account was opened with and we will help you regain access.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Moj paket → Nalog → Obriši nalog (My package → Account → Delete account). Step-by-step instructions are in the Account deletion document.
