2. What the app does

The app is a companion to the play centre service. With it you can:

  • show your child's QR code, which staff scan to record arrival and departure;
  • follow your hour package balance and any hours owed;
  • see the menu, the activity schedule, and announcements;
  • request a booking for a slot or a birthday party.

The app does not provide childcare and is not a means of supervising your child. The service itself is provided on the play centre premises, under its house rules.

4. The child's QR code

The QR code is the key to checking a child in and out. Do not share it with third parties and do not publish it. The first scan checks the child in, the next one checks them out. If you suspect the code has reached someone who should not have it, ask staff to issue a new one.

5. Hour packages, billing and payment

  • Hour packages are assigned by staff after payment. Payment does not happen in the app — the app only shows the balance.
  • Stays are billed under the rules the play centre publishes in the app and on the premises. If a stay is not covered by a package, the difference is recorded as hours owed and settled at the play centre.
  • A package’s expiry date is shown next to it and the package stops being valid once it passes.
  • The figures shown in the app are informational. In case of a discrepancy, the play centre's own records prevail and you can check them on site.

6. Bookings

A booking request sent through the app is not a confirmed booking. A booking is valid only once staff confirm it. Cancellation terms are agreed with the play centre.

7. Notifications
