Account deletion

Last updated: September 9, 2026

You can delete your Kids club account yourself, without contacting support. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete it in the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the My package tab.
  3. At the bottom of the screen, under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on the device.

What is deleted

  • your account: name, email address, phone number, password, profile photo;
  • your children's data: name, date of birth, gender, allergies and notes, QR code;
  • your children's arrival and departure history;
  • your hour packages and the record of hours used;
  • notifications sent to your account and your device token.

What remains and why

  • Bookings stay in the play centre calendar but are no longer linked to your account — the slot is already reserved in the schedule.
  • Records the law requires us to keep, primarily accounting documentation for paid packages, are kept for the period and to the extent the law prescribes.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your account, send a deletion request from the email address the account was opened with to kidsclubbw@gmail.com. We delete the account within 30 days at the latest, once we confirm the request came from the account owner.
