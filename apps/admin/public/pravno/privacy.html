2. What data we collect

Parent data

  • first and last name;
  • email address (also the username for signing in);
  • phone number, if you provide one;
  • password — stored only as a cryptographic hash, never as text;
  • profile photo, if you upload one.

Child data — entered by the parent

  • first and last name;
  • date of birth;
  • gender, if provided;
  • allergies and health notes, if provided. This is health data and receives special protection — see section 4;
  • the child's QR code, generated by the app and used to record arrival.

Play centre usage data

  • arrival and departure times and length of stay;
  • hour package balance, hours used, and any hours owed;
  • bookings: title and type of the slot, date and time, number of guests, child name and age, and a contact phone number, if provided;
  • notifications the play centre has sent you through the app.

Technical data

  • a token used to deliver notifications to your device (issued by the Expo service, see section 6);
  • ordinary server logs for each request (IP address, time, path) — created automatically and used for security and troubleshooting.

What we do not do. The app has no ads, no behavioural analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share data with third parties for marketing. We do not collect your location and do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

4. Child health data

The allergies and notes field is optional. If you fill it in, that data counts as a special category of personal data and we process it solely on the basis of your explicit consent, given by entering it. It serves one purpose only — so staff know how to act while the child is at the play centre.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing that field in the app or by contacting us at the address in section 1. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

5. Children's data

The app is intended for parents and guardians, not for children. Accounts are opened and child data is entered by an adult parent or guardian, who thereby also consents to the processing of their child’s data. We do not collect data directly from children and children do not create accounts.

6. Who else can access the data

  • Play centre staff — see the data needed to check a child in, calculate hours, and keep children safe, through an internal panel protected by a password.
  • DigitalOcean, LLC — server hosting provider. Data is stored on a server in a data centre in the European Union.
  • Expo Project, Inc. (USA) — delivers notifications to your phone. It receives your device token and the notification text, which may contain the child’s name and whether they were checked in or out. The transfer relies on standard contractual clauses and is necessary for the notification feature. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings and the app still works.
  • Apple and Google — deliver notifications to the device through their messaging systems.

We do not disclose data to anyone else, unless required by law or by a competent authority.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and child data — for as long as the account exists. When you delete the account, they are erased immediately and permanently.
  • Arrival and departure history — at most 2 years after the stay, to review attendance and resolve billing disputes. Erased sooner if you delete your account.
  • Accounting records — for the period required by law, including after account deletion, to the extent the law requires.
  • Server logs — short term, for security and troubleshooting.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app: My package → Account → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes your account, your children’s data, and their visit history. Instructions and the full list of what is deleted are in the Account deletion document.

9. Your rights

Under the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • access the data we process about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • data portability in a common electronic format;
  • withdraw consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Send requests to [POPUNITI: email address]. We reply within 30 days at the latest.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11120 Belgrade, Serbia, www.poverenik.rs.

10. Security

  • all traffic between the app and the server runs over encrypted HTTPS;
  • passwords are stored only as a cryptographic hash (bcrypt), never as text;
  • the sign-in token is kept in the phone’s protected storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android);
  • changing your password signs out every other device where the account was left open;
  • the staff panel requires a password and access is limited by user role.

11. Cookies and local storage

The mobile app uses no cookies. The staff panel uses browser local storage only for what is strictly necessary: the sign-in token, the theme choice, and the selected scanning camera. There are no tracking or advertising cookies, so no cookie consent is required.
